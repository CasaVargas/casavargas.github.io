Privacy Policy

Nimbus, by Casa Vargas LLC. Last updated November 13, 2026.

Nimbus is a Mac app made by Casa Vargas LLC. This policy covers the app and this website. The short version: the app sends us nothing, and the website counts visits without identifying you.

The app

Nimbus has no account, no sign‑in with us, no analytics and no crash reporting. Casa Vargas LLC does not receive your files, file names, cloud credentials or usage data, because the app never sends them to us.

What connectsWhenWhere it goes
rclone, to the cloud services you addWhen you browse, copy, back up or open a fileDirectly from your Mac to those services
rclone, to a service’s sign‑in pageDuring setup, for services that sign in on the webThrough your browser, to that service
Nimbus, to rclone.orgOnly if you ask Nimbus to download rcloneThe download is checked against rclone’s published checksums
Nimbus, to GitHubOnly if you turn on update checks or choose Check for UpdatesIt reads the update feed attached to the latest release

The cloud services you connect, rclone.org and GitHub each see the request your Mac makes to them, including your IP address, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

What stays on your Mac

Your sign‑in tokens and access keys are kept by rclone in its configuration file on your Mac. If you turn on Lock with Keychain, that file is encrypted and its password is kept in your login Keychain. Settings, transfer history and backup schedules are stored on your Mac as well. Removing the app and its files removes them; we hold no copy.

This website

This site counts page visits and clicks on the download links with Plausible Analytics, running on our own server. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser for tracking, and does not collect anything that identifies you. The download counter on the home page asks GitHub’s public API for totals from your browser, so GitHub sees that request.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and address for as long as it takes to answer and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and have none from the app to sell or share.

Children

Nimbus is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The history of this page is public in the Nimbus repository.
